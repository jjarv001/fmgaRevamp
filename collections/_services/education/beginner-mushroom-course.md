# Beginner Mushroom Course

Welcome to the Beginner Mushroom Course! This course is designed for anyone curious about the fascinating world of fungi. Whether you're a hobbyist, aspiring grower, or simply interested in learning more about mushrooms, this course will give you a solid foundation. You'll learn about the basics of mushroom biology, popular edible and medicinal strains, and the fundamentals of log-based cultivation—all presented in a clear, approachable way.
